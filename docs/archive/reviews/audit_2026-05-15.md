# NiftyShield Codebase Audit Report (Final)

This report summarizes the findings of a systematic 10-tier audit of the NiftyShield repository. Findings are ordered by increasing impact, with financial precision and protocol-level invariants identified as the highest-risk areas.

## Final Audit Verdict

The NiftyShield codebase is currently **unfit for production execution** due to pervasive "float contamination" and critical operational safety gaps. 

The **single highest-leverage fix** is **Finding [31]**: updating the `MarketDataProvider` protocol to return `Decimal` values instead of `float`. Because this protocol is the ingestion point for all market data, this one change will force type-safety through the entire downstream P&L pipeline, resolving findings [28], [29], and [30] simultaneously. 

Secondary priorities must include implementing the `DateAwareLotSizeResolver` (Finding [22]) and refactoring the `TelegramNotifier` (Finding [13]) to prevent event-loop starvation during market hours.
